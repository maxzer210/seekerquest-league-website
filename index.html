About the Project

What is
Seeker Quest League?

Seeker Quest League is a mobile-first tap-to-earn game built on the Solana blockchain. Players tap their way through quests, earn OIB tokens, and compete in seasonal leagues for real SOL prizes.

With the Genesis Pre-Season now live, early players get exclusive OIB allocations, NFT badges, and priority access to future seasons — the earlier you start, the more you earn.
