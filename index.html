What is Seeker Quest?

A Living World
of Challenges

Seeker Quest is a competitive adventure league that merges strategy, exploration, and skill into a single ever-evolving experience. Players take on the role of Seekers — elite agents tasked with uncovering secrets across procedurally-generated realms.

Earn points, climb the leaderboard, and claim legendary status in your division. New quests drop every week, each with unique mechanics and rare rewards.
